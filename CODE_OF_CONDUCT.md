# Code of Conduct

Aeon is built by people with very different backgrounds and experience. Everyone who takes part (issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions) is expected to make it a place where others can do their best work.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Give feedback that is specific and kind; accept feedback without taking it personally.
- Say what you do and do not know. A clear "I did not check this" is welcome; a confident guess is not.
- Respect other people's time, privacy and credit. Do not share anyone's private information.
- Welcome newcomers. Explain, do not belittle.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, discrimination, threats, sexual attention or imagery, publishing private information, deliberately disruptive behaviour, and spam. Using the project to attack or surveil people is not acceptable either.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and may warn, temporarily block or permanently block anyone who breaks these rules. To report a problem, use GitHub's "Report content" on the item, or write to the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately and in good faith.

This is a short code written for this project. It follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
